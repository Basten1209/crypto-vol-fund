# Monthly Portfolio Backtest Note

## Setup

- Rebalance frequency: first available date of each month.
- Information set: portfolio for month M is generated from the Phase 4 forecast whose origin date is the previous day, e.g. 2026-01-01 uses information through 2025-12-31.
- Holding windows: first 7 days or first 14 days of each month.
- Off-window days are treated as cash with 0% return in the aggregate Phase 6 performance table.
- Single-asset cap: 25%.
